Its overall silhouette is squat and dome-like, broader than it is tall, with a softly bulging top that rolls down into a slightly narrower, more irregular base; the form is rounded rather than angular, the result of long weathering rather than fresh fracture. The body is not a smooth sphere but a lumpy, undulating volume broken by shallow concavities, rounded shoulders, and a few flatter facets where the stone has spalled or been worn. The dominant surface materials are three: bare exposed rock, living moss, and crusty lichen. Together they give a damp, shaded, long-undisturbed impression.
