A lush, herbaceous foliage plant built as a dense basal rosette of large, simple lance-to-paddle-shaped leaves erupting from a short clustered crown at ground level, with no visible woody trunk. Texture is supple and fleshy, catching highlights along the ridged veins. This kind of plant belongs in warm, humid, tropical or subtropical understory settings, lush garden beds, conservatories, or as a potted interior specimen prized for bold, sculptural foliage.
